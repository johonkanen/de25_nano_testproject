Loop details

-

PI on the hottest sensor, once a second; the fan duty stays within 30..255 (below 30 the fan can stall). The fan can only cool: at minimum fan and still below the setpoint, or at full fan and still above it, the loop is saturated and says so. If the controller stops, the fan keeps its duty and is raised to 128 after 10 s; the fan chip's own over-temperature fail-safe stays on.
